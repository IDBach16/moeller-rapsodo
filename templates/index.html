    Stuff+ — {{ player.name }}

    Pitch quality graded by a model trained on MLB run values, using only what the unit measures (velo, spin, break, release, and each pitch against his own fastball). 100 = the average Moeller tracked pitch; every 10 points = one staff standard deviation. It grades the pitch's shape, not command — on our own charted games it tracks whiff rate and says nothing about strike rate, which is exactly what Stuff+ should do. Under 10 tracked reps = provisional.
